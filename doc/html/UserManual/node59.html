ARTMAP_Synchronous

  The first step is to calculate the output value of the input units (input units of ARTa, ARTb). Now a complete propagation step takes place, i.e. all units calculate their output and activation value. The search for two recognition neuron with highest activation follows. The search takes place in both ARTa and ARTb. The required parameters are in field1, field2, field3 respectively.
